The program

Three waves — measure first, then structure, then deep verification

WAVE 1Seven decisive improvements + the feedback pulse

Each one small, independently shippable, and immediately visible in output: traceability that survives export, gaps labeled instead of silent, decks written to be read cold. Ships with the in-product readiness pulse — the measured baseline every later claim is judged against.

Weeks · 7 items + measurement backbone
WAVE 2Eight structural lifts — the visible step-change

Frameworks and differentiators gated by the evidence that fills them; deck structure validated against the RFP's actual asks; retrieval unified and reranked; tables ingested without losing their headers; an automated fact-check reading every slide against its sources.

1–2 months · 8 items
WAVE 3Six deep-verification builds, each stage shippable

Every figure extracted as a typed claim and machine-matched to its source; derived numbers computed by code, never improvised; the deck plan checked as an argument structure; a per-slide audit trail answerable in one query.

Staged · 6 items

Structure before verificationWave 2 produces the checked plan that Wave 3's machine verification attaches to.
