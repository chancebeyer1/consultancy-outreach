# ICP — asbestos & lead abatement contractors (notification filers)

Small to mid-size licensed abatement contractors: asbestos removal, lead abatement, mold
remediation, and the demolition firms that carry their own abatement crews. Roughly 3 to 150
employees, owner-operated or with a small office staff.

The person we want is whoever actually files the state notification before a job starts. In a
firm this size that is the owner, the operations manager, or a single office administrator who
does it alongside scheduling and invoicing. Job titles are unreliable here, so treat "who
submits the 10 day notice" as the real qualifier, not the title on the profile.

**Best fit: firms that work across state lines.** Every state runs its own portal and its own
rules, so a contractor working Massachusetts, Connecticut, and New York in the same month
carries three different filing processes. Massachusetts alone requires the notification to
reach both MassDEP and the Department of Labor Standards, charges $100 per notification, and
charges another $35 every time a job gets revised. Single-state firms feel a fraction of this.

Also a fit: firms doing high job volume with short lead times, where the ten working day
waiting period collides with a customer who wants work started sooner, and firms whose jobs
get rescheduled often enough that revisions are a routine cost rather than an exception.

**Not a fit:** building owners, schools, and facility managers dealing with AHERA
record-keeping. That is a different market with different software and different buyers.
Environmental consultants and testing labs are also out, unless they file notifications on
behalf of contractors, in which case they are worth one conversation.
