# Offer — PanelPath partnership (rev-share / white-label / operating partner)

## What PanelPath is (one breath)
Done-for-you insurance credentialing for California behavioral-health practices: we build and
authorize the CAQH profile, prepare and submit every payer application, chase it to approval,
and watch every renewal deadline forever. Practices get their OWN contracts — not paneled
under a marketplace's tax ID like Headway/Alma — so they set their own rates and keep the
contract if they leave. Unique hook: since Jan 2024, LMFTs and LPCCs can enroll in and bill
Medicare directly (via PECOS) — and the marketplaces don't do Medicare at all.

Live product: https://panelpath.vercel.app · pricing: $200 CAQH build + $300/payer/provider
enrollment + $99/provider/mo monitoring. Fulfillment is systemized (agents draft, a human
reviews and submits — never auto-submitted to a payer).

## The asks, by segment

**Billers (Segment A) — referral or white-label:**
- Referral: flat $75–150 per closed enrollment package, or 15–20% of first-year revenue.
- White-label: "YOUR-BRAND Credentialing, powered by us" — they sell at their price, we
  fulfill at list, they keep the spread and the client relationship.
- Positioning: we fix the thing that DELAYS their billing revenue; zero competitive overlap.

**Coaches / consultants / community owners (B, C) — affiliate:**
- 15–20% rev share or flat per-conversion fee; a free-enrollment giveaway for their audience
  as the activation ("we'll enroll one of your members free — including the new Medicare
  path for LMFTs/LPCCs").
- One email/post to test; we handle everything downstream; they see every dollar.

**CPAs / attorneys (D) — mutual referral:**
- We send entity/tax questions back. Simple two-way arrangement, no money needed to start.

**Credentialing specialists (E) — operating partner conversation:**
- Moonlight-friendly: rev-share per enrollment they run, path to a bigger role if the pilot
  cohort converts. They bring payer contacts + fulfillment chops; we bring software + demand.

## Voice / rules for drafts
- Peer-to-peer BD tone, not a pitch to a prospect. Lead with THEIR economics, not our features.
- Concrete numbers beat adjectives ($300/payer/provider; 15–20%; $75–150/referral).
- One CTA: a 15-minute call. Never attach decks. Never mention patient data (we never touch
  any). Administrative service, not legal advice — never imply otherwise.
- It's fine to say this is early ("I'm choosing 2–3 partners while my pilot cohort runs") —
  scarcity is honest here.
