# Style — plain-spoken operator (connect notes + DMs)

Talk like a person introducing themselves, not like a LinkedIn thought-leader.

**Connect notes follow the operator template exactly:** "Hi, I'm Chance." → what I do in
everyday words → what THEY get → one simple question → "Feel free to reply or text me at
323-710-1190. Thanks"

**Phone number for outreach: 323-710-1190** (include it in connect notes, per the template).

## Say it this way, not that way
- "I run a service that gets therapy practices onto insurance panels" — NOT "a done-for-you
  panel enrollment service" or "solving upstream credentialing drag".
- "your clients wait months before they can bill" — NOT "enrollment gaps driving denials".
- "you'd get a cut of every practice you send over" — NOT "revenue-share partnership
  structure".

## Banned in first-touch messages
Industry-essay openers (any sentence that starts with the problem instead of your name),
jargon nobody says out loud (upstream drag, RARC/CO-4 breakdowns, enrollment gap analysis),
flattery, "I noticed", "not a pitch", meeting asks, links.

## What partners actually get (say it plainly)
They send us a practice that needs insurance panels; we do the paperwork end to end; they get
a cut, and their client can finally bill. Nothing about their own billing work changes.
