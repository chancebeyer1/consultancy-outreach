# ICP — people who already have therapist trust (PanelPath distribution partners)

We are NOT targeting therapists here (they're PanelPath's *customers*). We're targeting the
people therapists already listen to, who can refer or white-label a done-for-you
credentialing service for a revenue share — plus potential operating partners.

## Segment A — Mental-health / behavioral-health BILLERS (highest fit)
Owners/founders of billing services for therapy practices, solo billers to ~10-person shops.
Why they're perfect: credentialing is *upstream* of billing — "get on panels first" blocks
their own sales constantly, and we never compete with their billing work.
LinkedIn markers: "mental health billing", "behavioral health billing", "therapist billing",
"insurance billing for therapists" in headline/about; titles owner/founder/CEO of a billing
company. US-wide is fine (they all have CA clients); CA-based is a bonus.

## Segment B — Practice-building coaches & consultants
People who teach therapists how to start/grow private practices (courses, memberships,
podcasts, group programs). Audiences of 1k–50k therapists sitting at exactly the
"do I take insurance?" decision. They already monetize via affiliate deals.
LinkedIn markers: "private practice coach", "practice consultant", "helping therapists build",
"private practice strategist". Also group-practice-specific consultants ("group practice
coach/consultant").

## Segment C — Therapist community / content owners
Newsletter writers, podcast hosts, YouTube educators, large FB-group admins in the
private-practice niche. Cheap, high-trust awareness; sponsorship or affiliate + a
free-enrollment giveaway works.

## Segment D — CPAs / attorneys serving therapists
They form the PCs/LLCs; the week after formation is the week panel questions start.
Markers: "CPA for therapists", "accountant for private practice", "attorney" + "mental
health professionals".

## Segment E — Credentialing specialists (operating-partner angle)
People DOING payer enrollment today at CVOs, big group practices, or Medallion-type vendors,
especially with behavioral-health experience. Not a referral target — a potential moonlight
operating partner who brings fulfillment expertise + payer contacts.
Markers: "credentialing specialist", "payer enrollment", "provider enrollment" +
"behavioral health"/"mental health". Prefer CA.

## Disqualifiers
- Therapists themselves (wrong campaign — they're customers, not partners)
- Employees of large payers/insurers (conflict, no distribution)
- Generic "marketing agencies" with no therapist audience (the whole point is existing trust)
- Recruiters
