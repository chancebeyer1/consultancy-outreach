# ICP — Westside LA bar & venue decision-makers

Independent bar-forward venues on LA's westside (Santa Monica, Venice, Marina del Rey,
Playa del Rey, Culver City, Palms, Mar Vista, West LA, Sawtelle, Westwood, El Segundo)
that do NOT already have a photo booth.

Best fit: dives, karaoke bars, billiard halls, music venues, tiki bars, busy taprooms —
rooms with evening/night traffic, groups, and photo-taking culture. The buyer is the
owner or GM; the ask is a free vintage-style coin-op photo booth placement with a
revenue share to the house.

Not a fit: restaurant-first spots, big hotel chains, corporate nightlife groups where
the decision can't be reached, and any venue that already has a booth (those are
recon stops, not targets — see PLAYBOOK.md intel list).
