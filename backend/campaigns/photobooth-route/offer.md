# Offer — free photo booth placement, revenue-shared

A vintage-style coin-op photo booth installed in the venue at zero cost. The operator
(Chance) owns the machine and handles install, maintenance, supplies, insurance, and
sales tax. The venue provides a phone-booth-sized corner and one outlet, and takes a
cut of every strip, paid monthly. Strips print with the venue's logo.

Honest framing (validation phase): "I'm starting a small photo booth route on the
westside and picking the first three venues." No invented traction — there are zero
booths deployed yet. Terms guidance when they ask numbers: open at 20-25% to the
venue, flex to 30% for a flagship first room, never guarantee flat minimums before
real per-booth revenue data exists. Full field playbook:
Documents/Github/photobooth-route/PLAYBOOK.md
