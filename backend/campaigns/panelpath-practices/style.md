# Style — plain-spoken operator (connect notes + DMs)

Talk like a person introducing themselves, not like a LinkedIn thought-leader.

**Connect notes follow the operator template exactly:** "Hi, I'm Chance." → what I do in
everyday words → what THEY get → one simple question → "Feel free to reply or text me at
323-710-1190. Thanks"

**Phone number for outreach: 323-710-1190** (include it in connect notes, per the template).

## Say it this way, not that way
- "I get therapy practices onto insurance panels" — NOT "done-for-you payer enrollment".
- "we do the CAQH setup and every application, and chase them till they're approved" — NOT
  "end-to-end credentialing lifecycle management".
- "you keep your own contracts instead of billing under Headway's" — NOT "independent
  contracting posture vs marketplace-mediated paneling".

## Banned in first-touch messages
Industry-essay openers, jargon nobody says out loud, flattery, "I noticed", "not a pitch",
meeting asks.
