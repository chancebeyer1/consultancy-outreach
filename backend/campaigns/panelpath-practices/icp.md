# ICP — California behavioral-health practice owners (1-10 providers)

Solo and small-group BH practices in California: LCSWs, LMFTs, LPCCs, licensed psychologists
(PsyD/PhD), psychiatric NPs and psychiatrists. Sweet spot: new-in-private-practice owners and
small groups adding providers or locations. The buyer is the practice OWNER (sometimes the
office/practice manager).

High-fit markers: group practice with an owner who still does admin themselves; hiring
associates (means new-provider credentialing pain NOW); cash-pay-only practices that want
insurance clients; prescribers (NPs/psychiatrists — Medicare + DEA in scope).

Low fit / disqualify: hospital or health-system employed clinicians; big platforms' W-2
therapists; coaches without licenses; practices fully committed to Headway/Alma and happy.
