## The wedge (real, verifiable — use these, never invent others)
- Practices get their OWN payer contracts — not paneled under a marketplace's tax ID like
  Headway/Alma — so they set their own rates, bill independently, and keep the contract.
- Since Jan 2024, LMFTs and LPCCs can enroll in and bill Medicare directly (PECOS) — and the
  marketplaces don't do Medicare at all.
- DIY reality: enrollment runs 10-20 hours per provider per payer; incumbent credentialing
  platforms price for health systems (tens of thousands per year), not a 3-therapist practice.
